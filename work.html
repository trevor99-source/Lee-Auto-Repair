Our work

Real problems, properly fixed

Here is a look at the kind of jobs that come through the workshop.

Bakkie that kept going flat

Problem: battery died every few days.

✔ Fix: tested the charging system, replaced the faulty alternator and checked charging after.

Lights lost power on the dash

Problem: dash and lights cutting out.

✔ Fix: traced a damaged section of wiring, repaired it and re-insulated the loom.

Lost-key emergency

Problem: the owner had only one key and lost it.

✔ Fix: programmed a new key to the car and made a spare as well.

Doors would not lock

Problem: central locking stopped working.

✔ Fix: replaced the faulty lock motor and tested the remote.

Headlights not working

Problem: one headlight kept going out.

✔ Fix: found the circuit fault, repaired it and checked both lights.

Warning light mystery

Problem: engine light on and the car felt weak.

✔ Fix: scanned the car, tested the sensor and fixed the real cause.
