Car won’t start

Clicking, dead silence or slow turning. Could be the battery, starter or wiring.

Battery keeps going flat

Something may be draining it, or the alternator is not charging.

Lost or locked-out keys

We can help with key programming and some lock-out problems.

Lights have failed

Headlights or brake lights gone out and it is not safe to drive.

Burning smell or smoke

Switch off safely. This can be an electrical fault.

Alarm stuck on

Alarm will not stop or the car will not start after it went off.

While you wait

Stay safe

  • Move to a safe spot and switch on your hazard lights
  • Don’t keep cranking the starter. It can make things worse
  • If you see smoke or flames, get out and away from the car and call 112 or 10111
  • Don’t jump-start a leaking or swollen battery
  • Stay visible and keep your phone charged
What to send us

  • What happened just before it stopped
  • Any warning lights or sounds
  • A photo or short video if you can
