Questions

Common questions

Do you work on my make of car?

We work on most makes and models. See the full list on the Services page, or send us your car details.

Will I know the price first?

Yes. We explain the fault and the price before we start work.

Can you come to me?

For breakdowns, message us your location through the Emergency callout page and we will tell you what is possible.

How long does a check take?

Simple faults can often be found quickly. Harder electrical faults take longer. We will give you an idea when you drop the car off.

Do I need to book?

Message ahead so we can plan time for your car.

I lost all my keys. Can you help?

Often yes, depending on the car. Send us the make, model and year and we will tell you.
