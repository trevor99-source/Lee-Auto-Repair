Our services

Everything your car’s electrics need

Not sure what’s wrong? No problem. Here is each service explained in plain English, with the signs to look out for.

Car diagnostics

Modern cars have a small computer that watches the engine, brakes, airbags and more. When something is wrong it stores a fault code and may turn on a warning light. We connect a scanner, read those codes, then test the car to find the real cause, not just the symptom.

  • Scan for fault codes on most makes
  • Test sensors, wiring and modules
  • Explain the problem in simple words

You might need this if: Warning lights on the dash, rough running, loss of power, high fuel use.

💡 Never ignore a warning light. A small fault caught early is cheaper to fix.

Wiring repairs

Wires carry power to every part of your car. Over time they can rub through, melt, corrode or get chewed by rats. We trace the broken wire and repair or replace it neatly so the problem does not come back.

  • Fault finding with proper test tools
  • Repair or replace damaged wiring
  • Neat, safe, properly insulated work

You might need this if: Fuses blowing again and again, items working on and off, burning smell, flickering lights.

💡 If you smell burning plastic, switch off and call us. Don’t keep driving.

Alternator and starter

The starter turns the engine over to get it running. The alternator charges your battery while you drive. If either one fails, you can be left stranded. We test them first and only replace what is truly faulty.

  • Test the battery, starter and alternator
  • Repair or replace faulty parts
  • Check charging after the job

You might need this if: Slow or clicking start, battery light on, dim lights, car dies while driving.

💡 A clicking sound is not always a flat battery. Let us test before you buy a new one.

Alarm and sound systems

We help with car alarms, immobilisers, remotes, speakers, amplifiers and radios. A working alarm helps keep your car safe, and good sound makes every drive better.

  • Fix alarms that go off for no reason
  • Remotes and immobiliser faults
  • Speakers, amps and radio fitting

You might need this if: Alarm sounding by itself, remote not working, crackling speakers, no sound.

💡 A random alarm can drain your battery overnight. Get it checked.

Lights

Headlights, indicators, brake lights and dash lights all keep you safe. When one stops working, the cause can be a bulb, a fuse, a switch or a wire. We find out which and fix it.

  • Headlight and tail light faults
  • Indicators and brake lights
  • Dash and interior lights

You might need this if: A light that will not come on, bulbs that keep blowing, dim or flickering lights.

💡 Driving without working brake lights is dangerous and can get you fined.

Central locking

Central locking lets one button lock every door. When it fails, one door or all of them can stop working. We repair the lock motors, wiring and remote system so everything works together again.

  • Fix door lock motors
  • Repair wiring and relays
  • Remote and key fob faults

You might need this if: One door will not lock, remote does nothing, doors lock or unlock by themselves.

💡 If a door will not lock, your car is at risk. Don’t wait.

Key programming

Many modern keys have a small chip that must be matched to your car. Lost your only key, or want a spare? We program new and spare keys so they start the car properly.

  • Spare keys programmed
  • Replacement keys after loss or damage
  • Remote and chip key matching

You might need this if: Lost your only key, a broken key, or the car will not recognise your key.

💡 Make a spare key now. Replacing a key after losing the last one costs more.

General service and repairs

Alongside the electrical work we do general servicing and checks to keep your car healthy. A regular service finds small problems before they become big, expensive ones.

  • General service and checks
  • Battery testing and replacement
  • Second opinions on faults

You might need this if: A service is due, a long trip is coming, or you want a second opinion.

💡 Ask us for a full check before a long trip.
